# Andalus Design

**Andalus Design by Diwan** — an open-source design system inspired by Islamic civilization, built for Flutter and SwiftUI.

**أندلس ديزاين** — نظام تصميم مفتوح المصدر مستلهم من الحضارة الإسلامية، لـ Flutter وSwiftUI.

---

## Why Andalus

Andalus is not only a component library. Every design decision — color, proportion, geometric pattern, motion, typography — is documented together with its reasoning and its cultural source, so anyone can study the language behind the system, not just use its parts.

The name is a symbol of civilizational depth, not a limit on style: the system draws on Islamic civilization broadly, not on Andalusian architecture alone.

## Principles

- **Bidirectional by design** — RTL and LTR are first-class; Arabic, Turkish, and English from day one.
- **Documented reasoning** — no token or component ships without its rationale.
- **Dignity over manipulation** — no dark patterns.
- **Foundations are not diluted** — contributions are reviewed against the system's Islamic foundations.

## Status

Early stage. The first consumer of Andalus is the [Diwan](https://github.com/Melsaeed276) platform.
